Privacy policy

vv studio · last updated 6 October 2026

vv studio is a private tool one family uses to make and publish YouTube Shorts on channels they own. This page explains what data it uses, why, and how to remove it.

Google and YouTube access

When a channel owner connects their channel, vv studio asks Google for permission to:

  • Upload videos to that channel (youtube.upload).
  • Manage the channel's videos: set thumbnails, change publish time or visibility, and read video details (youtube, youtube.force-ssl).
  • Read and reply to comments on the channel's videos (youtube.force-ssl).
  • Read YouTube Analytics for the channel's videos, read only (yt-analytics.readonly).

It uses this access only to do these things for the person who connected the channel, inside vv studio.

What is stored, and where

  • The sign-in token Google returns, so uploads can happen on schedule.
  • Each video's YouTube id, publish time and status, and snapshots of its views, likes, comments and analytics figures.

This is kept in vv studio's own database on the owner's computer or private server. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone outside the family members who use vv studio. Each member only sees their own channels.

Services vv studio uses

To make videos, vv studio sends the video's topic and script to Anthropic (Claude) for writing and fact-checking and to ElevenLabs for the voice, and searches Pexels for stock footage. YouTube data is passed on in two cases only, both started from inside vv studio:

  • When a person asks for a draft reply to a comment, that comment's text is sent to Anthropic's Claude to write the draft.
  • When suggesting new video topics, figures about how the channel's videos performed are sent to Anthropic's Claude.

vv studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect vv studio from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask the owner to delete your channel's data from vv studio, by email below; it is deleted within 30 days.
