vv studio

A private tool for making YouTube Shorts on our own channels.

vv studio is used by one family to run their own YouTube channels, such as Har Pehlu and The Versatile View. It helps write a short script on a topic, checks the facts, adds a voice, footage and music, and renders a vertical video. A person watches every video and approves it before anything is uploaded.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads the videos we approve to our own channel, as private videos scheduled to go public at a set time.
  • Sets each video's thumbnail and, when we ask, changes its publish time or visibility.
  • Shows our videos' views, likes and comments, and reads YouTube Analytics so we can see what works.
  • Lets us read and reply to comments on our videos.

It is not a public service and has no sign-up: only family members invited by the owner can use it, and each person only connects channels they own.
